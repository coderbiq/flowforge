# Triage labels and roles

Classify incoming external work as `bug` or `enhancement`, then use these coordination labels:

- `needs-triage`: classification or verification is incomplete;
- `needs-info`: a human or external source must supply a fact;
- `ready-for-agent`: the triage brief is clear enough to route into the engineering Skills;
- `ready-for-human`: only a human can perform the next external action;
- `wontfix`: rejected or outside scope.

These labels coordinate incoming issue triage. They are not proposal readiness gates and must not replace requirement/design gaps, DAG blockers, or ticket completion evidence.
